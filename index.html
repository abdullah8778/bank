    About the AI Engine

    HemoTrack's screening engine is a transparent, rule-based scoring model (v1.2) — not a machine-learning or diagnostic system. It combines four automated checks: (1) clinical thresholds for hemoglobin, blood pressure, age, and weight; (2) the minimum inter-donation interval calculated from this device's own donation history; (3) a keyword scan of free-text health notes flagging terms that warrant clinician attention; and (4) a 0–100 confidence score summarizing the above for faster staff triage. All logic runs locally in the browser — no data is sent anywhere.
