    How the Engine Works

    HemoTrack's engine is a transparent, rule-based weighted scoring model — not a black-box or deep-learning system. It runs four checks: clinical thresholds (hemoglobin, blood pressure, age, weight), the minimum inter-donation interval from this device's own history, a keyword scan of free-text health notes, and a 0–100 confidence score combining all four. Every weight above started from a clinical default and moves only in small, bounded steps — either typed in directly under Guidelines, or nudged automatically each time staff confirm or override an assessment in the Training Queue. Everything runs and is stored locally in this browser; nothing is sent to a server.
